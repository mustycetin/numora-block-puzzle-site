Numora: Block Puzzle

Numora: Block Puzzle is a calm number logic puzzle. Split the board into rectangles so that each one contains exactly one number equal to its area, across the Classic, Toplama (Sum), and Double modes. The app is ad-free, works offline, and stores all of your progress only on your device. See the privacy policy or visit support.
